----------------------------------------------------------------

Your meetings never have to leave your phone.

Recap records, transcribes, and summarizes meetings on your device by
default. No account, no subscription, and your meetings go to no cloud
service unless you turn one on.

PAY ONCE, OWN IT
Meeting AI assistants typically cost $100–200 every year, forever, and
send your conversations to their servers to earn it. Recap is a single
one-time purchase: everything below is included and works offline. No
tiers, no upsells.

RECORD ANYWHERE
• One-tap recording, transcribed on your phone
• Record device audio from webinars and videos (audio only, with your
  consent; keep the screen unlocked while recording)
• Import existing recordings and transcribe them in the background
• Crash-safe: recordings save as you go

TRANSCRIPTION THAT RESPECTS PRIVACY
• On-device Whisper models — from 75 MB Tiny to Large v3 Turbo
• NVIDIA Parakeet (25 languages) and Nemotron engines, also on-device
• Speaker detection with reusable voice profiles
• Custom vocabulary so your names and jargon come out right
• Spoken language detected or chosen; AI summaries follow it, or
  translate speech to English

AI SUMMARIES — EVEN OFFLINE
• Built-in AI engine runs models like Gemma, Llama, Qwen, and Mistral
  directly on your phone: summaries, action items, and Q&A, offline
• Llama models: Built with Llama. Models download on demand under their
  publishers' licences, shown in the app.
• Or connect your own endpoint (Ollama on your PC, Groq, OpenRouter)
• Enhance rough notes into complete minutes using the transcript
• "Catch me up" mid-meeting recap when you join late
• Clinical and legal summaries are drafts for professional review, not
  medical records or legal advice.
• Report problematic AI output from inside the app

FIND IT, FIX IT
• Search shows the words around each match and jumps to that moment,
  photo text included; find in meeting with next/previous
• Find & replace across a meeting, with undo, keeping tap-to-seek
• Free up space: remove old audio, keep transcripts; optional
  automatic clean-up

STAY ON TOP OF FOLLOW-UPS
• Action items with reminders, a cross-meeting follow-ups list, and
  export to your task app
• Pre-meeting briefs for recurring meetings, from the calendar nudge
• Weekly digest, tags, recurring-meeting grouping

BUILT FOR REAL WORK
• Word-level tap-to-seek playback, speed control, skip-silence
• Conversation insights: talk-time balance, questions, pace
• Rich notes with checklists, photos, whiteboard OCR, file attachments
• Share audio clips of key moments; mark one from the notification
• Markdown/PDF export, encrypted backups
• Biometric app lock and screenshot blocking

DEVICE REQUIREMENTS (estimates)
64-bit Android 8.0+ (arm64 or x86_64). Recommended: 4 GB RAM or more;
6 GB+ for on-device AI summaries with the recommended model.
RAM, minimum / comfortable:
• Transcription: Whisper Tiny 1/2 GB; Base, Small Q 2 GB; Small, Large
  v3 Turbo, Parakeet 3/4 GB; Nemotron 3.5 3/6 GB
• AI summaries: Qwen 2.5 0.5B 2/3 GB; Gemma 3 1B 3/4 GB; Qwen 2.5 1.5B,
  Qwen 3.5 2B 4/6 GB; Llama 3.2 3B, Gemma 3 4B, MedGemma 4B 6/8 GB;
  Gemma 4 E2B, Qwen 3.5 4B 8/10 GB; Gemma 4 E2B QAT 8/12 GB; Mistral 7B,
  Llama 3.1 8B, Gemma 4 E4B (also QAT) 12/16 GB; Qwen 3.5 9B 16/18 GB
• Speaker detection: 1–2 GB
Recap suggests models that suit your phone.

PRIVACY, CONCRETELY
Everything is stored in the app's private storage on your device. The
developer runs no servers and receives nothing unless you send it.
Transcription and AI run on your phone by default; Android's speech
service and your own AI endpoint are optional, and send data only if
you turn them on. Reading text in photos uses Google's ML Kit, which
sends Google diagnostic data about the device and app, never your
photos or meetings. Optional AI models (0.1–5.7 GB) download once, then
work offline. Recording laws vary by location — always record with
consent.

Recap is open source.
