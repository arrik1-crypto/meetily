----------------------------------------------------------------

Your meetings never have to leave your phone.

Recap records, transcribes, and summarizes meetings entirely on your
device. No account, no cloud, no subscription — your conversations stay
yours.

PAY ONCE, OWN IT
Meeting AI assistants typically cost $100–200 every year, forever, and
send your conversations to their servers to earn it. Recap is a single
one-time purchase: everything below is included, works offline, and
never phones home. No tiers, no upsells, no recurring anything.

RECORD ANYWHERE
• One-tap recording with live on-device transcription
• Record device audio for webinars and calls (audio only, with your consent)
• Import existing recordings and transcribe them in the background
• Crash-safe: transcripts save continuously while you record

TRANSCRIPTION THAT RESPECTS PRIVACY
• On-device Whisper models — from 75 MB Tiny to Large v3 Turbo
• NVIDIA Parakeet (25 languages) and Nemotron engines, also fully on-device
• Speaker detection with reusable voice profiles
• Custom vocabulary so your names and jargon come out right
• Translate-to-English mode for multilingual meetings

AI SUMMARIES — EVEN OFFLINE
• Built-in AI engine runs models like Gemma, Llama, Qwen, and Mistral
  directly on your phone: summaries, action items, and Q&A with zero
  network involvement
• Or connect your own endpoint (Ollama on your PC, Groq, OpenRouter)
• Enhance rough notes into complete minutes using the transcript
• "Catch me up" mid-meeting recap when you join late

STAY ON TOP OF FOLLOW-UPS
• Action items with reminders, a cross-meeting follow-ups list, and
  export to your task app (.ics or text)
• Pre-meeting briefs for recurring meetings: last time's outcomes and
  open items, right from the calendar nudge
• Weekly digest, tags, recurring-meeting grouping, full-text search

BUILT FOR REAL WORK
• Word-level tap-to-seek playback, speed control, skip-silence
• Conversation insights: talk-time balance, questions, pace
• Rich notes with checklists, photos, whiteboard OCR, file attachments
• Share audio clips of key moments
• Markdown/PDF export, encrypted backups
• Biometric app lock and screenshot blocking

PRIVACY, CONCRETELY
Everything is stored in the app's private storage on your device. The
developer runs no servers and receives no data. Optional AI models are
downloaded once (0.1–5.7 GB depending on your choice) and then work
offline. Recording laws vary by location — always record with consent.

Recap is open source.
